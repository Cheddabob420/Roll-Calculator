    How to Use

    1. Enter Total Run Length: Input the total footage of your run.
    2. Subtract Roll Footages: Each time you click "Next", enter the footage of the roll you're using.
    3. Monitor Remaining Footage: The display shows your remaining footage. It turns red when below 5000 ft so you know you will have enough time to get the next up.
    4. View Results: When you go negative, you'll see the final butt roll footage and whether it's suitable to return to storage.

    Color Indicators

    • Green - Butt roll is 8000 ft or more (good to return)
    • Red - Butt roll is under 8000 ft (too small, consider splitting another roll)

    How to Use Sheet Scrap & Tolerance Calculator

    1. Enter Total Order Sheets & Sheets Per Unit: The calculator locks in your 10% scrap allowance (and 90% minimum shippable sheets) and converts the order into unit counts.
    2. Enter Scrapped Sheets: Input the total damaged/scrapped sheets from the run to see what percentage of the order was lost.
    3. Check Tolerance & Makeup: If scrap stays at or below 10%, the banner stays green and shows your remaining scrap buffer. If it exceeds 10%, the banner turns red and shows the exact sheets and units needed to make up the shortage.
